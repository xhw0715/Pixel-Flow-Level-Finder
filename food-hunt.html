What Is Food Hunt: Pixel Puzzle?
Tap a color, release the ants, and eat every pixel clean

Food Hunt: Pixel Puzzle is a free mobile casual puzzle. The board is a pixel-art food scene. Tap any color to release a swarm of matching ants, then watch them cross the picture and eat every pixel of that color. The rule takes no tutorial, but a bad tap order can trap a color in a corner. Every level is a small exercise in planning the next move.

Core gameplay: tap a color, release ants, clear the board

The rules are simple. The tap order is the real puzzle.

1

Tap one color

Tap any colored pixel to release a matching ant colony from outside the board.

2

The ants eat matching pixels

The swarm moves across the picture and consumes every food pixel of its own color until none remain.

3

Clear every color

Finish the level by removing every color from the board without leaving any pixels behind.

Four steps for a clean clear

1

Scan the whole picture

Before tapping, find every color and its largest cluster. Ten seconds of observation can save most of a restart.

2

Clear the largest block first

The color with the most pixels takes longest to clear. Send it out early to leave more room for the swarms that follow.

3

Release one swarm at a time

Let each color finish before starting the next. If one color is blocked, clear the color in front of it first.

4

Eat the last pixels

The level ends when the last pixel is gone. If you get stuck, watch the solution and retry with a new order.

Expert tips for a stuck board

Largest cluster first

Clear the biggest color block before the small pieces. It keeps the board open for the swarms that need space.

Wait on scattered pixels

Do not tap a scattered color too early. Open the board first, then sweep all of its pixels in one clean run.

One tap, one clear

A few seconds of planning can save minutes of scattered tapping. Check which colors block each other before you start.

Restart when it jams

If four or five taps leave no clear route, restart. A clean board is faster than untangling leftover pixels.

Frequently asked questions

How do you play Food Hunt: Pixel Puzzle?

Tap a color to release matching ants. They cross the board and eat every food pixel of that color. Clear all colors to finish; there is no timer or move limit.

Is Food Hunt free?

Yes. The game is free on Android, supported by ads and optional purchases. You can finish every level without paying.

How many levels are there?

The game has hundreds of levels across Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, and Expert tiers. The guide currently has 1,190 complete video walkthroughs and written step-by-step notes.

Where can I find a level answer?

Search the Food Hunt Guide level library by level number. Each page has a YouTube walkthrough and a written tap order.

Why do I keep getting stuck on the same level?

Most dead boards come from tapping a color too early and trapping another color in a corner. Clear the largest block first, leave scattered colors for later, and remove the blocking color before opening a new front.

Does Food Hunt work offline?

Yes. After installation, normal play works without an internet connection.
