What Is Bus Traffic Fever!?
Match passengers, dispatch buses, and keep a station slot open

Bus Traffic Fever! is a free mobile queue-and-bus puzzle from GOODROID. Color-coded passenger groups rotate around a U-shaped queue toward one boarding entrance. Tap an accessible bus that matches the color at the entrance, send it into a station slot, and clear the lot before every slot fills with the wrong color.

Core gameplay: read the entrance, dispatch a match, free the slot

The rules are simple. The queue and the station row turn color matching into a planning problem.

1

Read the boarding entrance

The queue carries passenger groups past a single entrance. Only the color at that entrance can board right now.

2

Send the matching bus

Tap an accessible bus of the same color. It drives into an open station slot and begins loading its passengers.

3

Watch the counter and clear the row

The number under the bus counts down as passengers board. At zero it leaves, the slot opens, and the queue moves on.

Four steps to avoid the Out of Slot failure

1

Check the entrance before every tap

The useful color changes as the queue rotates. Re-read the entrance after each bus departs instead of repeating the previous move.

2

Keep at least one slot free

A free slot gives the next matching bus somewhere to land. A full row of colors that are not boarding is the usual way a run gridlocks.

3

Finish one color before opening another

Large passenger groups can need more than one bus. Clear the group in front before spending slots on a color further back in the queue.

4

Restart before the row fills

If every station slot holds the wrong color, the next match has nowhere to go. A clean restart is faster than rescuing a dead row.

Features and game mechanics

A one-finger parking puzzle with two constraints: the entrance color and the number of open station slots.

🔄 A rotating passenger queue

Groups move around a U-shaped queue toward one boarding point. A color that is far away now may be the next one you need.

🚏 Limited station slots

Every dispatched bus occupies a slot until its counter reaches zero. Sending a mismatched bus wastes time and space at the same time.

🎨 Duplicate colors are normal

Two buses of the same color are not a mistake. A long passenger group may need several loads before the entrance rotates.

📈 Two difficulty tiers

The indexed library has 99 Beginner levels and 141 Intermediate levels. Later boards use longer stops and tighter station space.

🆓 Free to play

The game is free on Google Play and the App Store, with optional purchases and ads depending on the store listing.

🧠 Order beats speed

There is no need to rush. The challenge is choosing which bus to dispatch and when to leave a slot empty for the next group.

Expert tips for a jammed station row

Do not park a color that is not boarding

A bus only loads passengers of its own color. A mismatched dispatch holds a slot for the full countdown without clearing the queue.

Use the first bus to buy room

The opening dispatch decides how much space the rest of the queue has. Prefer a move that clears passengers and returns its slot quickly.

Watch the gap between groups

A short group can leave the entrance before a long sequence begins. Use that window to clear a blocking bus instead of starting a new color.

Learn the level, not just the color

Screenshots and videos show the field layout as well as the queue. Watch the first bus enter, then copy the route from the same starting board.

Frequently asked questions

How do you play Bus Traffic Fever!?

Passengers rotate around a U-shaped queue toward a boarding entrance. Tap an accessible bus whose color matches the entrance; it drives into a free station slot, loads matching passengers, and leaves when its counter reaches zero. Clear the lot without filling every slot with the wrong color.

Is Bus Traffic Fever! free?

Yes. The game is free to download from Google Play and the App Store. It may show ads and offer optional in-app purchases, and every guide page is free to read without registration.

How many levels are there?

The guide currently indexes 240 levels, from Level 1 through Level 240. Of those, 99 are Beginner boards and 141 are Intermediate boards. Each listing has a board image, difficulty tier, and full video walkthrough.

Where can I find a level solution?

Search the Bus Traffic Fever Guide level library by level number. You can also open the 40-level range containing your board, from Levels 1-40 through Levels 201-240.

Why do I keep running out of station slots?

Most failed runs dispatch buses of a color that is not at the entrance. Those buses occupy slots without clearing the queue. Match the entrance color, keep one slot free, and restart early when the row is full of mismatched colors.

Does Bus Traffic Fever! work offline?

Once installed, normal play does not require a network connection. The guide itself needs a connection to load its pages, board images, and YouTube walkthroughs.
