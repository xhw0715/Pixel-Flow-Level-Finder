What Is CasualGames?
One hub for seven popular casual puzzle games and their level guides

mobilecasualgames.com is a guide hub for mobile puzzle games. It does not make games. Instead, it brings seven popular casual puzzle libraries into one place: choose the game, jump to the block containing your level, and open the matching video or written solution without bouncing between several fan sites.

The seven games in the hub

All are free mobile puzzles. Ratings and install counts come from their store listings.

🐜 Food Hunt: Pixel Puzzle

By EVERFUN. Tap a color to release matching ants that eat every pixel of that color. There is no timer or move limit; the entire challenge is the tap order. 1,000,000+ installs · 4.57 rating · Guide at food-hunt.org

🐜 Colony Flow!

By ABI GAMES. Guide ants along routes to carry and sort colored blocks. Clear the blocks and the hidden pixel picture slowly appears. One-finger controls, but longer solution chains. 1,000,000+ installs · 4.15 rating · Guide at colony-flow.org

🐱 Meowdoku: Brain Puzzle Games

By Oakever Games. Sudoku constraints meet Minesweeper-style deduction: one cat per colored region, one per row and column, and no cats touching. Solve by logic, not guessing. 10,000,000+ installs · 4.70 rating · Guide at meowdokuguide.com

🏖️ Sand Blocks: Drop Puzzle

By Rollic Games. Pull colored sand into matching slots to clear the board and reveal the picture beneath. The flow is relaxing; the later layouts are not. Physics falling-sand puzzle · Guide at sand-blocks.org

🎨 Pixel Flow!

By Loom Games. Route every color to its matching endpoint and untangle the pipes one by one. The pixel picture completes itself. Later boards get much tighter. 10,000,000+ installs · 4.51 rating · Guide at pixelflowonline.net

💥 Smash Fest!

By Flow Games. Aim and drop to clear each stacked block layout. Short rounds, direct feedback, and hundreds of levels with a steady difficulty climb. 1,000,000+ installs · 4.73 rating · Level pages hosted by CasualGames

💍 Rotate Rings

By Nebula Studio. Each board is a set of interlocking rings. Rotate them to align gaps and release the layers, from simple C-shaped locks to triangular frames. 1,000,000+ installs · 4.77 rating · Level pages hosted by CasualGames

How the guide is organized

Unlike aggregators that copy everything onto one site, CasualGames sends you to the place that actually maintains the guide.

One page per game

Open a game from the directory to see its full level list, split into blocks of 100 so you can jump straight to your range.

One video per level

Every one of the 8,019 indexed levels has a complete walkthrough video showing the route.

Direct links to dedicated sites

Food Hunt, Colony Flow, Meowdoku, Sand Blocks, and Pixel Flow each have a dedicated guide site, so level links go there instead of duplicating content.

Hosted pages when no site exists

Smash Fest! and Rotate Rings do not have dedicated guide sites yet, so CasualGames hosts their level pages itself.

Extra notes for stuck boards

When readers report a difficult level, the team adds a written sequence explaining why each move is made.

Free, no account

No paywall and no download wall. Guides and articles are open without registration or login.

Frequently asked questions

What is CasualGames?

An independent hub for casual puzzle guides. It indexes 8,019 levels across seven mobile games, with a video solution for each level and extra written notes for commonly stuck boards. It does not develop or publish the games.

Which games are included?

Food Hunt: Pixel Puzzle, Colony Flow!, Meowdoku: Brain Puzzle Games, Sand Blocks: Drop Puzzle, Pixel Flow!, Smash Fest!, and Rotate Rings. Each has its own page and 100-level navigation blocks.

How do I find a specific level?

Choose the game, jump to the 100-level block containing your level, then scan down. Levels with a dedicated written guide have a marker that opens the step-by-step notes.

Why do some level links leave CasualGames?

Several games already have dedicated guide sites maintaining the same solutions. To avoid duplicating content, CasualGames links directly to those level pages. Games without a dedicated site stay on CasualGames.

Does it cost anything or require registration?

No. Every guide and article is free to read, with no account, paywall, or download requirement.

Is CasualGames affiliated with the developers?

No. It is an independent fan-made guide. Game names, artwork, and store data belong to their respective owners.
