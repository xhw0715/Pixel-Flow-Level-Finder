What Is Block Out! - Color Sort Puzzle?
Slide colored blocks into matching doors and untangle moving routes

Block Out! - Color Sort Puzzle is a free mobile sliding-block puzzle. Every colored block must travel through open lanes to the door of the same color. Clear the board to finish. It looks like simple color matching, but it plays like a shared-route traffic puzzle: blocks borrow the same space, elevators change the lanes, and generators keep adding new blocks.

Core gameplay: find the doors, clear the lanes, move the blocks

The rules fit in three sentences. Choosing the next block is the hard part.

1

Read the doors and blocks

Each color has its own door. Count the blocks of each color and locate their doors before deciding which route must stay open.

2

Slide through empty lanes

A block can reach its matching door only when every cell on the route is clear. If another block is in the way, move that blocker first.

3

Clear the whole board

Blocks disappear when they enter the matching door. Send every colored block out and the level is complete.

Four steps to a cleaner board

1

Take a ten-second look

Do not slide the first block yet. Check the doors, the number of blocks in each color, and the open cells in the center.

2

Move the most constrained color

A color with one route or a nearly sealed door should move early. Colors with several open lanes can wait.

3

Keep the center open

Most blocks cross the middle of the board. Park spare blocks near the edges so they do not close another color's only route.

4

Plan around elevators and generators

Elevators change the route while they move, and generators add new blocks. Wait for the right cycle or clear the drop column first.

Features and game mechanics

Easy to learn and quick to play, with later boards demanding several moves of planning.

🛗 Moving routes

Elevators and block generators are not decoration. They interrupt the lanes mid-level and force you to choose when to move and when to wait.

🧩 One shared board

Blocks do not have private tracks. A careless long slide can seal the only door available to another color.

📈 Four difficulty tiers

Colors, lane density, and moving obstacles increase from Beginner to Expert. Early levels teach habits; later levels test planning.

📶 Offline play

Once installed, normal play does not require a network connection. Keep clearing levels on a commute or in airplane mode.

🆓 Free download

🧠 One minute to learn

Movement and color matching are obvious. Managing several colors in limited space is where the real challenge begins.

Expert tips for a jammed board

Move the fewest-route block first

Send out the block with only one usable route before other colors fill the board and that route disappears.

Never park in front of a door

Keep every door approach clear, even doors you do not need yet. One misplaced block can lock an entire color.

Finish one color at a time

Opening several fronts creates more half-cleared colors and more blockers. Concentrate on one route before starting another.

Restart a dead board early

If no remaining block has a complete route, keep testing will only waste time. Remember the layout and restart.

Frequently asked questions

How do you play Block Out! - Color Sort Puzzle?

Slide colored blocks through empty lanes and into the door of the same color. Blocks disappear when they reach the matching door; clear the board to finish.

Is Block Out! free?

The game is free to download and play on Google Play. Optional in-app purchases depend on the store listing, and every guide page here is free without registration.

How many levels are there?

The guide currently indexes 883 level numbers across four difficulty tiers, with 879 complete video walkthroughs. New levels are added as the game and video library update.

Where can I find a level solution?

Search the Block Out Guide level library by level number. Each page includes a board image, difficulty rating, and embedded video where available.

Why do I keep getting stuck?

Most dead boards come from moving a block into the central lane too early. Clear the most constrained color first, keep the middle open, and wait for moving obstacles instead of forcing a slide.

Does Block Out! work offline?

Yes. After installation, normal play works without an internet connection.
